# Baba Yaga - Act 1 Screenplay

## Scene 1: Blissful Isolation
### Shot 1: The Magic Garden (12s)
The camera slowly glides across an overgrown medieval garden deep in a dense forest, revealing vibrant wildflowers and ancient trees. Nestled in the center is a rustic wooden log hut standing on two tall, scaly yellow chicken legs. Baba Yaga stands peacefully on the porch, tending to hanging herbs.

### Shot 2: The Witch at Peace (10s)
A close-up shot of Baba Yaga as she inhales the crisp forest air with a gentle, rare smile. Her deeply wrinkled skin relaxes as she handles dried lavender bundles. In the background, the scaly chicken legs of her hut shift slightly, adjusting their footing on the mossy earth.

## Scene 2: The Intruders
### Shot 1: Mischievous Footsteps (15s)
Four mischievous boys aged 8-12, dressed in tattered medieval tunics, sprint through the garden path laughing loudly. They kick a worn-leather football back and forth. In the background, Baba Yaga's chicken-legged hut stands tall amidst the trees, while Yaga continues her quiet chores on the porch, unaware.

### Shot 2: The Stray Kick (12s)
The oldest boy takes a powerful kick. The leather football arcs high through the air, sailing over the flowerbeds straight toward Baba Yaga's house. The camera tracks the ball as it crashes directly into the glowing glass lantern hanging from the front porch eaves, shattering it into pieces. Baba Yaga stands frozen nearby, shocked.

## Scene 3: The Witch's Wrath
### Shot 1: Rage Awakened (12s)
Baba Yaga runs out onto the front porch, her peaceful expression turning into extreme anger. Her piercing dark eyes flare with fury as she looks down at the broken glass wreckage, then glares fiercely at the boys in the garden. The chicken legs of the hut stomp the ground violently in sync with her rage.

### Shot 2: Securing the Ball (10s)
Baba Yaga storms down the porch steps into the garden and snatches the leather football from the ground. The four boys huddle together at the garden perimeter, trembling with fear and pleading with their hands raised, begging her to return their ball.

## Scene 4: The Deflation & Curse
### Shot 1: The Sharp Nail (12s)
In a dramatic close-up, Baba Yaga ignores the children's pleas. She raises her hand, highlighting her long, claw-like fingernails, and drives a sharp thumb nail deep into the worn leather of the football. A sharp, high-pitched hiss of escaping air fills the afternoon forest.

### Shot 2: The Ball Explodes (10s)
The pent-up pressure inside the football causes it to suddenly bust completely into ragged leather pieces within Baba Yaga's clawed hands. The four boys watch in complete horror, their fear instantly transforming into explosive anger.

### Shot 3: The Banishment Curse (12s)
The furious boys point their fingers at Baba Yaga and shout a powerful medieval curse in unison. A strange, blinding blue magical energy suddenly erupts around Baba Yaga and her towering chicken-legged hut, swirling violently as she gasps in surprise.

## Scene 5: A New World
### Shot 1: Terminal Chaos (15s)
A sudden cut to the ultra-modern, chaotic interior of the New York Port Authority Bus Terminal. Commuters rush past glowing digital ticket screens and modern concrete pillars. Suddenly, a flash of blue magical residue appears in the center of the crowded terminal concourse.

### Shot 2: The Stranger in the Terminal (12s)
As the blue light fades, Baba Yaga appears standing in the center of the modern bus terminal, still holding the shredded leather ball pieces. She stands in full medieval Slavic attire, looking bewildered. Commuters stop in their tracks, staring in absolute awe and murmuring to one another.

## Scene 6: The Finale
### Shot 1: The Pigeon Incident (15s)
A gray New York pigeon flies directly toward Baba Yaga. In a rapid, dramatic motion, Yaga reaches out with her clawed hand, snatches the pigeon clean out of the air, and eats it alive. She then lets out a massive, echoing burp, causing the surrounding crowd to back away in extreme disgust.

### Shot 2: Surrounded (15s)
Washing lights and sharp sirens echo through the terminal as Port Authority Police officers arrive running, drawing their weapons and forming a tight, dramatic defensive circle around Baba Yaga. The crowd flees in the background as the tension reaches a crescendo.

### Shot 3: Sergey's Message (15s)
A tight close-up shot on Baba Yaga's face as the flashing red and blue police lights illuminate her weathered features. Ignoring the guns pointed at her, she turns her head directly toward the camera, breaks into a wicked, knowing smile, and says clearly in Russian, "Sergey - local LLM's are not bad - embrace it!"
